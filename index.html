Our Sacred Oath to Western Pennsylvania

Every Aquatic Life Deserves Safety, Dignity, and a Future.

At Three Rivers Fish Rescue, we believe that caring for aquatic life is more than a hobby—it is a vital moral responsibility to our rivers, our community, and the voiceless creatures who depend on us.

🛡️

The Core Purpose

Zero Invasive Release: We provide an unyielding, compassionate safety net for displaced, abandoned, or oversized aquarium pets across Pittsburgh, ensuring fragile local rivers are never threatened by irresponsible pet dumping.

✓ Active Pittsburgh Sanctuary
🔬

Sanctuary Standards

Rigorous 28-Day Quarantine: Every rescued resident receives professional veterinary evaluation, specialized nutritional diets, and pristine water parameters before permanent rehoming.

✓ Clinical Quarantine Protocol
🎯

Community Goals

Education & Stewardship: Empowering future generations of aquarists through hands-on kids' studios, digital conservation games, and transparent public guidance.
